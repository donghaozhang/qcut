# Xiaohongshu Publishing Package

## Ground the Writing

Read the corrected transcript and source notes before writing. Extract the hook,
the speaker's lived experience, three concrete beats, the strongest metaphor or
punchline, and the closing question. Do not invent product behavior, benchmark
results, dates, or personal experiences.

Match the speaker's voice. Use familiar sayings and metaphors to make technical
ideas tangible, but keep the factual sentence underneath them clear.

## Titles

Write three distinct options, then recommend one. Aim for roughly 14–22 Chinese
characters when the topic allows it.

Useful structures include:

- product or idea + real experience + unexpected consequence;
- contrast: “会造世界，也会拆墙”;
- familiar saying turned sideways;
- a concrete promise followed by a playful warning.

Keep one idea per title. Avoid fake urgency, unverifiable superlatives, and a pile
of punctuation. One well-earned emoji may work better than three.

## Body

Use this rhythm:

1. Open with the most vivid result or tension.
2. State what the speaker actually tried or observed.
3. Develop three short beats with concrete examples.
4. Zoom out to the wider implication.
5. End with a genuine question or invitation to compare experiences.

Use 俗语, 比喻, and 好玩感 selectively. Favor images people can picture: building a
house, opening a blind box, borrowing a chef, handing someone the keys, or fixing
one wall while another falls over. Avoid generic hype such as “太震撼了” unless
the transcript earns it.

Keep paragraphs short for phone reading. Include 6–10 relevant hashtags, mixing
the exact topic, product category, creator perspective, and platform context.

Save the package as:

```markdown
# 推荐标题

...

## 备选标题

1. ...
2. ...

## 正文

...

## 话题

#...
```

## Cover

Create an exact 1080×1920 portrait cover. Use:

- one exact main headline;
- at most one short kicker;
- the speaker or central subject in the lower half;
- the headline in the upper half with strong small-thumbnail contrast;
- a restrained palette of 2–4 colors;
- enough negative space to keep the design readable.

Prefer a real, flattering frame of the speaker. Use generated imagery for symbolic
backgrounds, diagrams, or playful supporting elements, not to falsify identity or
product evidence.

Use this sequence:

1. Extract several real frames and choose one with clear eyes, natural hands, and
   enough negative space.
2. Lock the exact main headline and optional kicker before generation.
3. Provide the selected frame as an identity reference; ask the model to preserve
   the person, pose, clothing, and recognizability.
4. Generate or edit the supporting design in portrait orientation.
5. Inspect every Chinese character, face, hand, logo, and crop at full resolution.
6. Export the final asset at exactly 1080×1920 and inspect it again as a small
   thumbnail.

If generated Chinese typography is unreliable, generate the visual treatment
without final lettering and composite the approved text deterministically. Never
ship misspelled cover text. If a model returns 2:3, crop only when the composition
has safe margins.

Reject a cover when it has misspelled text, too many elements, weak hierarchy, a
face hidden by type, or a promise stronger than the video.

## Copy Acceptance

Before delivery, confirm:

- every factual claim is supported by the transcript or a cited source;
- the recommended title matches the video's real payoff;
- the first two lines work without expanding the post;
- the sayings and metaphors sound conversational rather than pasted in;
- hashtags are relevant and not repeated in the prose;
- the cover text exactly matches the approved headline;
- the final cover dimensions are exactly 1080×1920.
