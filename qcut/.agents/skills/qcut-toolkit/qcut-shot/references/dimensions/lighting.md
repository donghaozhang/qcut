# Lighting Dimension

## Options

### natural

- believable ambient light
- soft realism

### bright

- crisp, commercial, polished
- clean highlights and minimal shadow ambiguity

### dramatic

- clear contrast
- controlled shadows and directional emphasis

### low-key

- darker frame with selective highlights
- noir and tension-friendly

### neon

- stylized colored practicals
- saturated accents and nightlife energy

### soft

- diffuse light
- gentle contrast and forgiving tones
