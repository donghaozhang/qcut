# Movement Dimension

## Options

### locked-off

- Stable camera
- Intentional, composed, observational

### handheld

- Slight natural instability
- Intimate and immediate

### dolly

- Smooth forward or backward push
- Emphasize reveal or commitment

### slider

- Controlled lateral movement
- Useful for product, detail, and elegant transitions

### crane

- Elevated or descending movement
- Useful for scale and spatial reveal

### dynamic

- Aggressive, kinetic motion
- Use for impact beats, conflict, or heightened tension
