## How to Use This List

1. **Connect on LinkedIn with a one-liner** about what you built at the hackathon. Don't send a wall of text.
2. **Prioritize the three ⭐ picks** if you're in AI/Video.
3. **Start with Tier 2** — they move faster and are more accessible at seed stage. Tier 1 is for when you have traction.
4. **SOSV sent two people** (Mohan + Parikshit) — that signals real interest in this space. Worth a deeper look.
5. **Civilization Ventures also sent two** — when a small fund sends two partners, they're serious.
